About Rave

Where the night never ends.

The vibe of Astana

Rave Astana is more than a bar — it's a full nightlife experience that combines karaoke, a chilled lounge, and an electric sport bar under one roof. Located in the heart of Astana, we're the city's go‑to spot for everyone who loves loud music, cold cocktails, and unforgettable nights.

Our space is designed for every mood. Sing your heart out in our private karaoke rooms, watch live football, UFC and NBA broadcasts on giant screens in the sport zone, or relax with friends in the lounge while our DJs spin the freshest tracks.

From birthdays and corporate parties to spontaneous Friday nights — Rave is built for friends, fun and unforgettable memories. Neon lights, premium sound, top‑shelf bar and a welcoming team. Step in, the night is yours.
